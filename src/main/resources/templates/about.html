About EcoRecycle

We are dedicated to making plastic recycling easy, efficient, and rewarding.

Our Mission

EcoRecycle connects households and businesses with certified recycling centers, making it easier than ever to dispose of plastic waste responsibly. Our platform rewards users for their eco-friendly actions while ensuring proper waste management.

What We Do

  • 🏠 Connect users with local recycling centers
  • 📅 Schedule convenient pickup times
  • ⭐ Reward eco-friendly behavior with points
  • 📊 Track your environmental impact
  • 🌱 Promote sustainable waste management

Why Choose Us?

Traditional recycling can be complicated and time-consuming. EcoRecycle simplifies the process by bringing recycling services directly to you. Whether you're a household or a business, we make it easy to do your part for the environment.
